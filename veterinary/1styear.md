### **1. Veterinary Anatomy** (Credit Hours: 4 + 3)



#### **Theory**

* **Unit 1: General Osteology, Arthrology, Myology, Angiology, Neurology, Splanchnology & Imaging**


* **General Osteology & Arthrology:** Structure, classification, and properties of bones. Classification of joints, diarthrodial joint structure, and movements.


* **General Myology:** Classification of muscles, etymology, tendons, ligaments, aponeuroses, synovial bursae, and sheaths.


* **Angiology, Neurology & Aesthesiology:** Heart structure, systemic/pulmonary circulation, lymphatic/venous systems. Central, peripheral, and autonomic nervous system architecture; meninges, brain, spinal cord, spinal nerve formation, and sense organs.


* **Surface Landmarks:** Palpable bony prominences, lymph nodes, arteries, surface veins for venipuncture, sites for bone marrow aspiration and CSF collection.


* **General Splanchnology & Diagnostic Imaging:** Boundaries of thoracic, abdominal, and pelvic cavities; topographies of digestive, respiratory, urinary, endocrine, and reproductive systems. Principles of radiography and ultrasound for soft tissue and bone.




* **Unit 2: Fore Limb**


* Bones of the ox forelimb compared with horse, dog, pig, and fowl. Structure of ox and horse hoof.


* Joints, ligaments, stay apparatus, major blood vessels, nerves, veins, and lymph nodes. Sites for radial, median, ulnar, and volar nerve blocks.




* **Unit 3: Head and Neck**


* Cranial and facial bones, cervical vertebrae of ox (compared with horse, dog, pig, fowl).


* Boundaries of oral, orbital, nasal, and cranial cavities; paranasal sinuses.


* Muscles of face, mastication, eye, ear, tongue, pharynx, soft palate, hyoid, and larynx.


* Teeth (dentition & age determination), hard/soft palate, salivary glands, thyroid, parathyroid. Jugular furrow boundaries, carotid sheath, sense organs, trachea, and esophagus.


* Surgical landmark sites for tracheotomy, esophagotomy, Stenson’s duct ligation, guttural pouch access, horn amputation, and regional cranial nerve blocks (cornual, mental, mandibular, maxillary, infraorbital, supraorbital, orbital, auriculopalpebral).




* **Unit 4: Thorax**


* Thoracic vertebrae, ribs, and sternum of ox (compared across species).


* Thoracic joints, ligaments, vessels, nerves, and lymph nodes. Organs: trachea, thymus, esophagus, lungs, heart, pleura, pleural reflections, and mediastinum.


* Auscultation/percussion areas for heart and lungs; paracentesis thoracis site.




* **Unit 5: Abdomen**


* Bones, joints, ligaments, nerves, and blood supply to abdominal viscera.


* Peritoneal reflections; comparative anatomy of digestive, urinary, male/female reproductive systems, spleen, major veins, lymphatics, and endocrine glands.


* Mammary glands in cow, mare, bitch, and sow.


* Clinical boundaries of flank and paralumbar fossa. Sites for liver/gallbladder/cecal biopsies, laparotomy, rumenocentesis, rumenotomy, abomasotomy, splenectomy, cystotomy, cesarean section, enterotomy, and paravertebral block.




* **Unit 6: Hind Limb and Pelvis**


* Pelvic and hindlimb bones, joints, ligaments, vessels, nerves, and lymph nodes of ox (compared across species).


* Pelvic viscera and peritoneal reflections; external genitalia. Inguinal canal boundaries, spermatic cord, and prepubic tendon.


* Sites for tibial, peroneal, plantar, and pudic nerve blocks, patellar desmotomy, urethrotomy, castration, vasectomy, epidural anesthesia (cranial/caudal).




* **Unit 7: Histology**


* Cytology, cell junctions, and basic tissue types (epithelial, connective, muscular, nervous, blood, bone marrow).


* Microscopic architecture of digestive, circulatory, urinary, respiratory, nervous, lymphatic, endocrine, male/female genital systems, sensory organs, and integument.




* **Unit 8: Embryology**


* Gametogenesis, fertilization, cleavage, egg types, morula, blastulation, gastrulation, implantation, and twinning.


* Foetal membranes in mammals/birds; placental classification.


* Germ layers and organogenesis of digestive, respiratory, urinary, circulatory, lymphatic, nervous, musculoskeletal, endocrine, and reproductive systems.





#### **Practical**



* **Cadaver Dissection & Demonstration:** Full cadaveric dissection of ox/buffalo calf (and comparison with other species) covering muscles, nerves, blood vessels, and internal organs of forelimb, head & neck, thorax, abdomen, hindlimb, and pelvic cavity.
* **Osteology & Arthrology:** Hands-on study of skeletons, bones, and joint preparations across domestic species.
* **Surface Anatomy & Live Animal Demos:** Palpation of bony prominences, lymph nodes, arteries (facial, femoral, coccygeal), veins (cephalic, jugular, saphenous), nerve block locations, and paracentesis/biopsy sites.
* **Histology & Micrometry:** Tissue processing, paraffin sectioning, H&E staining, light/electron microscopy comparison, and slide evaluation of all organ systems.


* **Embryology:** Examination of foetal membranes, placentas, congenital anomalies, and organogenesis slides.

### **2. Veterinary Physiology** (Credit Hours: 4 + 1)



#### **Theory**

* **Unit 1: Blood, Cardiovascular, Nervous and Muscular Systems**


* **Blood:** Composition, RBC metabolism, hemoglobin structure/derivatives/synthesis, hemostasis, hemorrhage, and blood groups.


* **Cardiovascular:** Heart morphology, cardiac excitability/conduction, cardiac cycle, cardiac output regulation, coronary circulation, blood pressure controls, capillary exchange, ECG, and echocardiography.


* **Muscle Physiology:** Neuromuscular transmission, action potentials, contraction mechanisms (excitation-contraction coupling), fatigue, and rigor mortis.


* **Nervous System:** Resting membrane potential, nerve impulse propagation, degeneration/regeneration, synaptic transmission, reflexes, posture/movement control, ANS, sleep-wake cycles, learning, memory, EEG, and sense organs (eye optics/photochemistry, ear hearing/vestibular apparatus, olfaction, taste).




* **Unit 2: Digestive and Respiratory Systems**
* **Digestive System:** Monogastric vs. polygastric morphology. Prehension, rumination, vomition, defecation. Salivary, gastric, pancreatic, and biliary secretions. Rumen microbial digestion, volatile fatty acid production, enzymatic digestion, intestinal transport/absorption, and detoxification mechanisms. Avian digestion.


* **Respiratory System:** Mechanics of breathing, gas transport, fetal/neonatal oxygenation, oxygen-hemoglobin dissociation, neural/chemical regulation of respiration, surfactant dynamics, compliance, hypoxia, and high-altitude adaptations. Avian respiration.




* **Unit 3: Excretory and Endocrine Systems**


* **Excretory System:** Nephron function, GFR determination, tubular reabsorption/secretion, urine concentration mechanisms, micturition, acid-base balance ($H^+$ regulation), fluid therapy principles, and body fluid dynamics (CSF, synovial fluid). Avian excretion.


* **Endocrine System:** Hormone-cell receptor interactions, hypothalamo-hypophyseal axis, thyroid, parathyroid, pancreas, adrenal, pineal, prostaglandins, calcium homeostasis, and endocrine disorders.




* **Unit 4: Reproduction, Lactation, Growth and Environmental Physiology**


* **Reproduction & Lactation:** Endocrine control of gonadal development, estrous cycles, oogenesis, spermatogenesis, fertilization, pregnancy diagnostics, parturition endocrine events, mammary gland development, lactogenesis, galactopoiesis, and milk synthesis.


* **Growth & Adaptation:** Biochemical/genetic determinants of growth, growth curves, recombinant technologies. Thermoregulation (panting, shivering, sweat), heat tolerance, climate variables (temperature, humidity, altitude), and animal behavior (neurophysiology, social/mating behavior).





#### **Practical**

* **Hematology:** Blood collection, anticoagulants, RBC/WBC counts, DLC, hemoglobin estimation, PCV, ESR, platelet count, clotting/bleeding time, erythrocyte fragility, and blood grouping.


* **Cardiovascular & Neuromuscular Demos:** Recording ECG, blood pressure measurement (sphygmomanometry), and nerve-muscle/heart physiology simulation experiments.


* **Digestive & Respiratory:** Rumen motility counting, VFA & ammonia nitrogen estimation in rumen liquor, rumen microbe counts, in-vitro digestive enzyme actions, spirometry, and respiratory parameter recording.


* **Excretory & Endocrine:** Urine analysis (normal/pathological constituents), GFR estimation, titratable acidity, inorganic phosphorus, and hormone bioassays/estimations.


* **Reproductive & Environmental:** Estrous phase detection (vaginal cytology/mucus), sperm motility/count/live-dead ratio, measuring body surface area, recording vital health signs (TPR), and environmental parameter measurement.

### **3. Veterinary Biochemistry** (Credit Hours: 2 + 1)



#### **Theory**

* **Unit 1: General Veterinary Biochemistry**


* Biological membranes, transport mechanisms, Donnan equilibrium, pH, buffers, Henderson-Hasselbalch equation.


* **Carbohydrates:** Structure and significance of monosaccharides, disaccharides, polysaccharides, and mucopolysaccharides.


* **Lipids:** Classification, biological significance of simple/compound/derived lipids, lipoproteins, fat indices, and prostaglandins.


* **Proteins & Amino Acids:** Amino acid properties, amphoteric nature, peptide bonds, protein structure levels, and biological classification.


* **Nucleic Acids:** Chemistry of purines, pyrimidines, nucleosides, nucleotides, and DNA/RNA structures.




* **Unit 2: Intermediary Metabolism**


* **Enzymology:** Enzyme classification, coenzymes, isoenzymes, active sites, kinetics, factors affecting activity, inhibition (competitive, non-competitive, allosteric), and clinical enzyme units.


* **Biological Oxidation:** Electron transport chain (ETC), oxidative phosphorylation, inhibitors, and uncouplers.


* **Metabolic Pathways:** Glycolysis, Kreb’s (TCA) cycle, HMP shunt, gluconeogenesis, glycogenesis, glycogenolysis, $\beta$-oxidation of fatty acids, ketogenesis, fatty acid biosynthesis, protein transamination/deamination, urea cycle, and purine/pyrimidine metabolism.




* **Unit 3: Veterinary Analytical Biochemistry**
* Disorders of carbohydrate metabolism (Diabetes mellitus, bovine ketosis, pregnancy toxemia, piglet hypoglycemia).


* Diagnostic clinical enzymology, liver function tests (LFTs), kidney function tests (KFTs - BUN, creatinine, uric acid), dysproteinemias, plasma lipid profiles, acid-base balance disturbances, biochemistry of shock/oxidative stress, fluid therapy rationale, and xenobiotic detoxification (Cytochrome P450).





#### **Practical**



* **General:** Preparation and standardization of volumetric solutions/buffers, pH titration curves, qualitative tests for carbohydrates/proteins, acid number of fats, and amino acid estimation (Sorensen's method).


* **Metabolism & Assays:** Enzyme activity assays (effects of temp & pH), quantitative estimation of blood glucose, protein, calcium, phosphorus, magnesium, ascorbic acid, milk lactose, flame photometry ($\text{Na}^+/\text{K}^+$), and paper/TLC chromatography of amino acids.


* **Clinical Diagnostics:** Pathological urine analysis, serum ALT/AST assays, A/G ratio, total serum cholesterol, BUN, serum bilirubin (direct/indirect/total), and interpretation of clinical chemistry panels.

### **4. Livestock Production Management (LPM)** (Credit Hours: 4 + 2)



#### **Theory**

* **Unit 1: General Livestock Management**


* Livestock demographics & contribution to the Indian economy. Animal husbandry terminology, body conformation, body condition scoring (BCS), identification/tagging techniques, culling, judging, and livestock show preparation.


* Routine management practices: disinfection, isolation, quarantine, carcass disposal, drug administration routes, prevention of vices, transport of livestock, and organic farming concepts.




* **Unit 2: Fodder Production and Conservation**


* Importance of grasslands/fodders; agronomical practices for leguminous and non-leguminous seasonal fodders. Soil/water conservation, green fodder preservation (silage/hay making), scarcity fodders, feed storage structures, and animal waste recycling.




* **Unit 3: Ruminant Management (Cattle, Buffalo, Sheep, Goat)**


* Housing design, building layout, orientation, and low-cost construction materials for Indian agro-climatic conditions.


* Breeds & economic traits of cattle, buffalo, sheep, and goat.


* Management schedules for neonates, calves, heifers, pregnant/lactating/dry animals, and breeding bulls. Draught animal management, male buffalo rearing for meat, clean milk production, wool/meat production management, weaning, and lamb/kid fattening. Farm records and accounting.




* **Unit 4: Zoo Animals Production Management**


* Wild and zoo animal taxonomy, habitat and enclosure housing requirements, captive feeding schedules, physical restraint, capture methods, zoo classification, national parks/sanctuaries management, and wildlife acts.




* **Unit 5: Animal Welfare, Ethics and Jurisprudence**


* Concepts of animal welfare and ethics. Role of AWBI, PCA Act 1960, CPCSEA guidelines, livestock transport rules, pet/companion/working animal welfare, disaster management for animals, legal duties of veterinarians, and veterinary code of conduct.




* **Unit 6: Poultry Production Management**


* Indian poultry industry status, avian species classification, commercial broiler/layer strains, and backyard/rural poultry systems.


* Housing systems (deep litter vs. cage), environmental control, brooding management, grower/layer/broiler/breeder management, poultry nutrition, biosecurity, and vaccination schedules.




* **Unit 7: Diversified Poultry & Hatchery Management**


* Incubation and hatchery practices (egg collection, candling, sexing, hygiene, hatchability factors). Management of ducks, geese, turkeys, quails, and guinea fowl. Integrated/contract poultry farming and poultry waste management.




* **Unit 8: Laboratory, Rabbit & Pet Animal Management**


* Care, housing, feeding, and breeding standards of laboratory animals (mice, rats, guinea pigs, hamsters; SPF/germ-free concepts).


* Commercial rabbit farming (breeds, kindling care, feeding, housing).


* Care, feeding, grooming, training, utility, and show standards for major dog breeds, cats, and pet birds.




* **Unit 9: Swine, Equine, Camel, Yak & Mithun Management**


* Swine: Breeds, breeding practices, housing, feeding, and management of piglets, growing stock, and farrowing sows.


* Equines: Breeds, dentition/ageing, grooming, stable management, shoeing/hoof care, feeding, saddling, colic prevention, and foal care.


* Camels, Yaks & Mithuns: Breeds, unique physiological/productive traits, high-altitude adaptations, feeding, and breeding routines.





#### **Practical**

* **General Livestock:** Farm tool identification, body part recognition, marking/tagging/branding/chipping, knot & halter making, dentition & age determination, body weight estimation via measurements, and project proposal preparation.


* **Fodder:** Fodder crop identification, seed/manure/fertilizer study, fodder conservation (silage/hay), and fodder cost economics.


* **Ruminant Farm Practices:** Farm layout planning, breed identification, humane handling/restraint, shearing, dipping, spraying, milking techniques, heat detection, calf care, and farm record keeping.


* **Poultry & Hatchery:** Avian anatomy study, egg quality evaluation, brooder setup, feed formulation, hatchery equipment handling, and poultry farm visits.


* **Lab Animals, Pets & Swine/Equines:** Handling, sexing, weaning, and feeding of lab animals/rabbits; dog/cat grooming and care; swine/equine restraint, stable routines, and horse riding basics (walk, trot, canter).
